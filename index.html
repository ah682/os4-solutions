08 / FAQ

The practical questions.

Still deciding whether the brief fits? Start here, or send the messy version of the problem directly.

hello@os4solutions.tech ↗
What kinds of projects do you take on?

Focused software builds, product features, cloud and delivery improvements, platform work, and reliability problems with a clear business reason behind them. If the scope is still fuzzy, discovery can be the first piece of work.

Can you work with an existing team?

Yes. OS4 can work as an embedded engineer alongside product and engineering teams, or independently on a well-bounded stream of work. Communication and ownership are adapted to your existing cadence.

Do you handle both application code and infrastructure?

Yes—that connection is central to the service. Product behaviour, deployment, cloud infrastructure, and observability are treated as one delivery system rather than separate hand-offs.

How does an engagement begin?

With a short conversation about the outcome, current system, constraints, and urgency. You then get a recommended first step: a focused diagnostic, a defined project, or an embedded engagement.

Can you help with a system that is already in trouble?

Yes. A stabilisation engagement starts by making the current state and highest risks visible, then works from the most consequential failure point outward. Urgency does not have to mean guesswork.
